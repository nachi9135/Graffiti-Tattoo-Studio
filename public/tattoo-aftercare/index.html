How to Take Care of Your New Tattoo

Proper tattoo aftercare is an important part of the healing process. Follow the aftercare instructions given by your tattoo artist and keep your new tattoo clean and protected while it heals.

1. Follow Your Artist's Instructions

Your artist may recommend a particular healing method depending on the tattoo, placement, size and type of dressing used.

Always follow the specific instructions provided by your tattoo artist. If your artist has applied a protective tattoo film, follow their instructions regarding when it should be removed or changed.

2. Keep Your Tattoo Clean

Basic Cleaning Steps

  • Wash your hands before touching your tattoo.
  • Gently clean the tattoo as instructed by your artist.
  • Avoid aggressive rubbing or scrubbing.
  • Pat the area dry with a clean, soft towel or let it air dry.

3. Moisturize as Recommended

Once your tattoo is ready for moisturizing, use the product and amount recommended by your tattoo artist.

Avoid applying unnecessarily thick layers of product. The goal is to keep the tattoo comfortable while following the healing instructions provided by your artist.

4. Things to Avoid During Healing

  • Do not scratch or pick at peeling skin.
  • Do not deliberately remove flakes or scabs.
  • Avoid unnecessary touching of the tattoo.
  • Avoid soaking the tattoo in water while it is healing.
  • Avoid activities that may excessively irritate the tattoo.
  • Protect the tattoo from unnecessary sun exposure during healing.

5. Tattoo Peeling and Itching

New tattoos commonly change in appearance as the skin heals. Peeling and itching can occur during the healing process.

Do not scratch or pull peeling skin. Allow the skin to heal naturally and continue following your artist's aftercare instructions.

6. Protect Your Tattoo From the Sun

Once the tattoo has healed, protecting tattooed skin from excessive sun exposure can help maintain the appearance of the tattoo over time.

Follow your artist's advice regarding sun protection and products suitable for your skin.

7. When to Seek Medical Advice

If you are concerned about unusual or worsening symptoms during the healing process, contact a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

Tattoo aftercare information does not replace professional medical advice.

Frequently Asked Questions About Tattoo Aftercare

How long does a tattoo take to heal?

Healing varies depending on the individual, tattoo size, placement and other factors. Follow the aftercare instructions provided by your tattoo artist.

Can I scratch my new tattoo?

Avoid scratching or picking at a healing tattoo. Follow your artist's recommended aftercare routine instead.

Can I touch my new tattoo?

Wash your hands before touching the tattoo and avoid unnecessary touching during the healing process.

Can I swim with a new tattoo?

Avoid soaking a fresh tattoo while it is healing. Ask your tattoo artist when it is appropriate to return to swimming or other water-related activities.

What should I do if I am worried about my tattoo?

If you have concerns about the healing process or symptoms that are unusual or worsening, seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

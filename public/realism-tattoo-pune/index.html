Detailed Tattoo Art

What Is a Realism Tattoo?

Realism tattooing focuses on creating artwork with detailed shading, proportions, contrast and visual depth. Designs can be inspired by photographs, people, animals, objects or meaningful subjects.

The final tattoo design is planned according to the subject, size, body placement and the level of detail required for the tattoo.

Our Process

Planning Your Realism Tattoo

A good realism tattoo starts with choosing the right reference and understanding how the design will work with your chosen body placement.

  • Discuss your tattoo idea and reference.
  • Choose the preferred tattoo placement.
  • Discuss the appropriate tattoo size.
  • Review the level of detail required.
  • Plan the tattoo according to the chosen design.

During consultation, our artists can discuss the practical considerations of your selected design.

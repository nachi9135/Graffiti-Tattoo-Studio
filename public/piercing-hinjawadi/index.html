Why Proper Piercing Preparation Matters

Before getting a piercing, discuss the placement, jewellery choice and aftercare instructions with your piercer. Understanding the healing process can help you take appropriate care of the piercing afterward.

  • Discuss the desired placement before the procedure.
  • Ask about appropriate jewellery for the piercing.
  • Follow the aftercare instructions provided by your piercer.
  • Avoid unnecessary touching while the piercing is healing.
  • Keep the piercing clean according to the recommended aftercare routine.

Piercing Aftercare

Proper aftercare is an important part of caring for a new piercing. Follow the specific instructions provided by your professional piercer.

Basic Aftercare Reminders

  • Wash your hands before touching the piercing.
  • Avoid unnecessary touching or rotating the jewellery.
  • Keep the area clean according to your piercer's instructions.
  • Avoid activities that unnecessarily irritate the piercing.
  • Do not remove jewellery without professional guidance during healing.

Fine Line Tattooing

What Is a Fine Line Tattoo?

Fine line tattoos use delicate lines and detailed artwork to create a clean and refined tattoo style. They can be designed around small symbols, lettering, flowers, illustrations, meaningful objects and other personalised concepts.

The final result depends on factors such as tattoo size, placement, design detail and the individual's skin. During consultation, the artist can discuss an appropriate approach for your design.
